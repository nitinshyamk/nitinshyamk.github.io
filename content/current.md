I'm currently work on [Hadrian](https://www.hadrian.co/)'s [DFM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_for_manufacturability) products. I spent my first three years at Hadrian building our automated [CMM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinate-measuring_machine) inspection capabilities initially as an IC, then as the TLM for a team of six. We covered two domains in Hadrian's mixed operational, physical, and digital domain with guarantees on high precision and human safety compliance: automated inspection routine generation, and distributed mechatronic robotic systems. My responsibilities spanned novel product design, system architecture, team and operational system management, computational geometry, custom parametrized CAD modeling techniques, full stack web services, and distributed systems.
